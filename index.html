Island

How to play

You are shell seekers on a tidal island. Collect shells and bring them back to your boat before the sea catches you!

On your turn

Roll the die and move up to that many hexes, one step at a time. You can’t walk on water. Step on a shell to pick it up. You can carry 3 shells at a time.

Your boat

In your boat, you move on water. On foot, you move on land. If you start your turn in your boat, you can row across any water hex (even flooded land), then step out onto dry land with the steps you have left. Your boat stays where you leave it.

Shells only count once you climb back into your boat, wherever it is. Climbing in ends your move. Shells you are still carrying at the end are worth nothing.

If the tide goes out and leaves your boat on dry land, it is beached. You can still climb in to bank, but it can’t row until the water comes back.

Shell values

Low beaches (heights 1–4) have 3-point shells. Middle ground (5–8) has 2-point shells. High ground (9–12) has 1-point shells.

The tide

After everyone moves, roll the tide die (1–4). The tide moves that far. It keeps coming in until high tide, then turns and goes out until low tide. Any hex with a height at or below the tide is underwater.

If the water reaches you, you’re swept away: you drop the shells you carry and wash up on the nearest dry land. When the tide goes out, it sometimes leaves new shells behind.

Winning

The game ends when the tide has gone out for the last time. Most banked points wins.

Shells still being carried don’t count.
